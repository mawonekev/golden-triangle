# Golden Triangle Victoria Falls

> **Victoria Falls Premier Adventures, Safaris & River Journeys**  
> Cloned from [Shearwater Victoria Falls](https://www.shearwatervictoriafalls.com/) with an exhaustive 60-activity database, moving picture background atmosphere, and real-time multi-currency booking engine.

---

## 🌟 Overview & Key Features

* **Moving Pictures & Background Video**:
  * Seamless full-width looping ambient background video in the hero banner.
  * Ken Burns cinematic moving pictures slideshow with smooth pan and zoom cross-dissolve transitions.
  * Interactive **Moving Scene Switcher** (Falls Aerial, Bridge & Bungee, Elephants, Rapids, Helicopter, Sunset Cruise).
  * Ambient Video Controls (Play/Pause, Sound On/Off) and Mosi-oa-Tunya mist particle spray animation.
* **Exhaustive 60-Activity Catalog**:
  * **Bridge Adrenaline (8)**: 111m Bungee Jump, Solo/Tandem Bridge Swing, Solo/Tandem Bridge Slide (106 km/h Zipline), Big Air Triple Combo, Historic Bridge Tour Catwalk, Bridge Café.
  * **Guided Tours & Scenic Hikes (6)**: Rainforest Guided Tour (16 viewpoints, raincoats included), Batoka Gorge Hike & Ndebele Homestead Brunch, Walking Safari with Armed Ranger, Rural Village Cultural Tour, Simunye Award-Winning Theatre Show, Big Tree & Town Heritage Tour.
  * **Elephant Immersion & Conservation (4)**: Through the Eyes of an Elephant Morning Walk, Afternoon Sundowner Walk, Family Viewing Platform, Elephant Walk & Campfire Bush Dinner Combo.
  * **Scenic Helicopter Flights (3)**: Flight of Angels (12-13 min), Explore the Zambezi (22 min), Zambezi Spectacular (25 min).
  * **River Cruises (5)**: Riversong Luxury Sunset, Classic Zambezi Sunset, Early Morning Sunrise Breakfast, Gourmet Lunch Cruise, Starlit 4-Course Dinner Cruise.
  * **White Water Rafting & Jet Boat (4)**: Full Day Grade V Rafting, Multi-Day Rampage Gorge Expedition, Adventure Jet Boat Extreme (465hp twin V8), Jetboat Plus Combo.
  * **Safaris & Wildlife (6)**: Chobe National Park Full Day Excursion (Botswana), Morning & Afternoon 4x4 Game Drives, Full Day Deep Safari, Spotlight Night Safari & 3-Course Bush Dinner.
  * **Discounted Specials & Combos (10)**: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum (The Full Monty), Gruesome Twosome 1 & 2, Ticket to Adventure 1 & 2, Family Explorer, Adrenaline Overdrive.
  * **Transfers & Shuttles (5)**: VFA Airport to Hotel, Hotel to VFA Airport, Return Shuttle Package, Cross-Border Zambia / Livingstone, Cross-Border Botswana / Chobe.
  * **Dining Experiences (5)**: The Boma Feast & Drumming, Rainforest Café Lunch, Bridge Café, Explorers Village Restaurant & Bar, Marula Café.
  * **Lodging & Stay (4)**: Explorers Village Deluxe Chalets (4-Star), Standard Rooms (3-Star), Serviced Safari Dome Tents, Campsite Pitches.
* **Live Interactive Utilities**:
  * Real-time Multi-Currency Conversion (USD, EUR, GBP, ZAR).
  * Instant search and 11 category tab filters with dynamic counts.
  * Interactive Booking Drawer with live guest calculation, optional add-ons, and simulated WhatsApp direct confirmation.

  * Detailed specifications modal with quick facts, inclusions, exclusions, and departure slots.

## 🚀 Running Locally

```bash
# Start local static server on port 3030
node server.js
```

Visit `http://127.0.0.1:3030` in your web browser.

## 🎨 Tech Stack & Design System
* **HTML5**: Semantic tags (`<header>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<dialog>`, `<aside>`, `<footer>`).
* **Vanilla CSS**: Pure custom design system without TailwindCSS. Deep African Obsidian (`#0B131F`), Safari Gold (`#D4AF37`), Sunset Ochre (`#C85A32`).
* **Typography**: Google Fonts (*Cinzel* display & *Plus Jakarta Sans* UI).
* **Vanilla JavaScript**: Zero external frontend dependencies, high performance.
